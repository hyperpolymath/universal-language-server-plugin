== What D142 means here

. The converter in `server/src/core.rs` plus
`server/src/formats/{yaml,xml,toml}.rs` is *self-contained and stays
that way*. No `docmatrix` crate, binary, submodule, service call-out,
or shared-converter client may be introduced without a fresh owner
ruling that supersedes D142 by name.
. "`Universal document conversion`" in this repo's tagline means
*editor-delivered* conversion: LSP 3.17 over stdio for the seven thin
editor clients, plus the HTTP REST API, WebSocket events, and web
dashboard served by the same Rust server.
. Boundary *documentation and links* are required and are not
dependencies. This file, the `README.adoc` boundary section, and
`.machine_readable/6a2/ECOSYSTEM.a2ml` carry the same truth in three
renderings; if they ever disagree, this file governs and the others
must be re-aligned to it.

== Suite map (docmatrix#71, summarised — canon lives there)

The authoritative suite contract is
https://github.com/hyperpolymath/docmatrix/issues/71[docmatrix#71]. What
follows is a pointer summary, not a restatement of canon:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Component | Boundary

| *This repo* (`universal-language-server-plugin`)
| One server, all editors. Own embedded converter; LSP/HTTP/WebSocket
delivery. No docmatrix dependency (D142).

| https://github.com/hyperpolymath/docmatrix[docmatrix]
| Multi-format conversion and precision infrastructure for the document
suite; owns the versioned interchange contract and precision
coordination.

| https://github.com/hyperpolymath/formatrix-docs[formatrix-docs]
| Tabbed views of one logical document across TXT,
delimiter-selected tabular text, Markdown, AsciiDoc, Djot, and A2ML;
synchronised editing is the intended progression.

| https://github.com/hyperpolymath/blocky-writer[blocky-writer]
| Fitting and placement into fixed-layout PDF and application-form
boxes, baselines, and per-character cells designed for hand spacing.
Not in the conversion business at all (their words, `#96`).

| ForthWall (proposed)
| Optional, capability-bounded execution layer for critical operations;
never a mandatory end-user product or runtime.
|===

Composition rules we inherit from `docmatrix#71` and honour:

* Each component remains useful standalone; composition is explicit and
user-selected.
* No supported workflow requires Microsoft Word (or any other mandatory
hub format) as an intermediate.
* The user's native input stays authoritative unless they explicitly
select another representation; lossy or ambiguous transformations are
proposals requiring approval, never silent normalisation.
* Proof obligations do not transfer: our conversion round-trip tests
prove our converter, nothing else.

== What would require re-ruling

Any of the following re-opens the boundary question and must be raised
as a new row on the standing decision surface (`standards#787`
successor) rather than implemented directly:

* Adding a docmatrix dependency (library, service, submodule, or
interchange-client).
* Implementing the suite's versioned interchange contract as a
*mandatory* path instead of an opt-in composition.
* Routing any supported workflow through a mandatory hub format.
* Changing this repo's tagline or description in a way that claims the
suite precision-infrastructure remit.
